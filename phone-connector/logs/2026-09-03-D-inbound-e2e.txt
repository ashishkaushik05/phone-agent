== LATENCY ISSUE (to investigate, not a Phase-D blocker) ==
  ~9.5s from Gemini setupComplete to the agent's first spoken token; 20-30s gaps mid-call.
  Director/tool-calls RULED OUT — they run off the audio path (phone<->Gemini WSS only).
  Prime suspect: phone's cellular/wifi quality to generativelanguage.googleapis.com
  (user reported 'really bad network'). Contributing, fixable on our side:
   - audioOutQueue is unbounded: after a network stall Gemini bursts buffered audio and
     we play all of it at realtime -> reply lands 10-20s late. Cap the queue / drop stale.
   - audio-in and audio-out share one WSS: loss on one direction head-of-line-blocks both.
   - AudioTrack setBufferSizeInBytes(minBuf*4) adds output latency; could tighten.
